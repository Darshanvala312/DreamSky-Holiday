Booking, Cancellation & Terms and Conditions

Everything that applies to a DreamSky Holiday booking, written out in full — so there are no surprises before, during or after your trip.

What makes us different from others?

A team of professionals working round the year, purely to make the experience during your trip a better one.

We don't just build holiday packages — we create holiday dreams.

Instead of a travel agency, we'd rather be called a travel friend — helping you choose the right place, the right hotel and the best package.

Only a human can understand the need of another human, so our specialised team is there before, during and after the trip — 24×7.

  • According to government regulations, a valid photo ID has to be carried by every person above the age of 18 staying at the hotel. The identification proofs accepted are Driver's License, Voter Card, Passport and Ration Card. Without a valid ID the guest will not be allowed to check in.
  • The breakfast menu is standard / fixed, and anything ordered extra shall be chargeable as per the regular rates.
  • Meals (if included in the package) will be served as per the hotel's policy only.
  • The primary guest checking in to the hotel must be at least 18 years of age. Children accompanying adults must be between 1–12 years.
  • The inclusion of an extra bed with a booking is facilitated with a folding cot or a mattress as an extra bed.
  • The room tariff includes all taxes.
  • The amount paid for the room does not include charges for optional services and facilities such as room service, mini bar, room heater, snacks or telephone calls. These will be charged at the time of check-out.
  • Modified bookings will be subject to availability and the revised booking policy of the hotel.
  • No discounts or deductions are applicable on the tariff whatsoever, even if the customer chooses to skip meals.

The initial payment required to confirm your booking depends on how far ahead of the travel date you book.

30 days or morebefore travel Airfare + 40% of the land package as initial payment
29 to 11 daysbefore travel Airfare + 60% of the land package as initial payment
10 days or lessbefore travel 100% of the package cost is required

Full payment is required at the time of booking for non-refundable components such as visa, cruise, NRF hotels and NRF flights.

Refund on cancellation strictly depends on the cancellation policy below.

30 days or morebefore travel Airfare + 40% of the land package — non-refundable
29 to 11 daysbefore travel Airfare + 60% of the land package — non-refundable
10 days or lessbefore travel 100% payment — non-refundable

If you wish to amend or change trip dates or passengers, the currently booked package will be treated as a cancelled booking and cancellation charges will apply as per this policy.

Itinerary & transport

  • Transportation provided will be as per the itinerary only, and not at disposal.
  • The customized package does not include guide services.
  • AC will be switched off in hilly areas.
  • The package itinerary is given for your sightseeing idea only; if any service or inclusion is not mentioned in the package inclusions, it will not be provided.
  • Day-wise itinerary plans and sightseeing can be changed internally, or cancelled, if there are any unavoidable circumstances in a particular place.
  • Any meals and services not mentioned in the itinerary are not included.
  • The package does not include gala dinner charges.

Images & hotels

  • Images shown in the itinerary or anywhere in the package only highlight the places. Your day-wise plan or itinerary can be different from the image, even if the image appears alongside a specific day. Images can also vary due to season, festival or any other situation.
  • A hotel image shown in the package is from the time it was taken, so it may differ from the current condition of the hotel.
  • You will be out sightseeing most of the time, so we prefer hotels that are convenient — which sometimes means they are away from the city-centre location.
  • In case of unavailability in the mentioned hotels, alternate accommodation will be arranged in a similar category hotel.
  • Hotel rooms will be given as per the hotel's standard check-in time only. Any early check-in or late check-out charges will be borne by the customer on a direct payment basis.
  • Some hotels may ask for security deposits, which have to be paid by the customer directly at the time of check-in and are refunded at the time of check-out.
  • DreamSky Holiday is not responsible if any services or amenities are suddenly unavailable at the hotel during your stay.
  • Rude or unprofessional behaviour by hotel staff does not come under DreamSky Holiday and we will not be responsible for the same.
  • The condition of hotel rooms or vehicles is sometimes not in our control, so DreamSky Holiday is not responsible for such situations.

Flights, visa & documents

  • Airline seats and hotel rooms are subject to availability at the time of booking.
  • If, in the case of confirmed tickets, the customer is denied boarding by the airline due to reasons beyond our control, DreamSky Holiday will not be responsible and no claim whatsoever can be made against the company.
  • Please note that low-cost carriers will be on hand baggage only; the same can be added by the customer directly with the airline.
  • Carry-baggage limits, meals and beverages on flights will be as per airline standards.
  • The passport should have a minimum validity of 6 months for your travel. The passenger must hold a valid passport and the required documents for visa purposes at the time of booking. In case of discrepancy, DreamSky Holiday reserves the right to dishonour the booking with no refund.
  • Visa processing and approval depend on the embassy, where DreamSky Holiday does not have direct access. In case of visa rejection, DreamSky Holiday is not responsible for any visa charges or for cancellation charges of flights or holiday bookings arising from the rejection.
  • In case of flight cancellation or rescheduling, DreamSky Holiday will not be responsible for any claims of refund for flights or holiday bookings. We will support you with alternate options, but losses arising from such situations will be on a direct payment basis by the customer.

Pricing, liability & jurisdiction

  • All rates are subject to change without prior notice as per availability and the final date of travel.
  • Rates are subject to change in case of any changes in airport taxes, hotel rates, rate of exchange (ROE) or transport rates.
  • Refund on cancellation strictly depends on the cancellation policy.
  • All baggage and personal belongings remain your responsibility. We will not be responsible for any such losses during the trip.
  • Any damage done to hotel premises or to any vendor's or partner's property will be borne by the customer only.
  • If your package needs to be cancelled due to weather conditions, natural calamity or any unavoidable situation where the customer is unable to travel, DreamSky Holiday will act as a responsible travel agency and try to provide the maximum possible refund, but this will depend on our policy and our agreements with vendors.   • If the customer fails to make payment as per our booking and payment policy, DreamSky Holiday reserves the right to cancel the booking. Refunds will be processed as per policy, if applicable.
  • DreamSky Holiday reserves the right to change, modify or terminate an offer at any time at its own discretion and without prior notice.
